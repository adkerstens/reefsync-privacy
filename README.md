# Privacy Policy – ReefSync

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the ReefSync Android application (“ReefSync”).

ReefSync is an aquarium lighting application designed to help reef aquarium hobbyists simulate natural reef lighting conditions and translate these into lighting schedules for supported aquarium lighting systems.

## Data collection

ReefSync does not require users to create an account or sign in.

ReefSync does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Settings and aquarium-related selections used by ReefSync are processed locally on the user's device unless otherwise stated.

## Internet access and external services

ReefSync may use an internet connection to access information or services required for certain app functionality.

Network requests may necessarily transmit technical information such as an IP address to the service being contacted. ReefSync does not use this information to create user profiles or for advertising.

## Google Play Billing

ReefSync offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock premium Export functionality.

Purchases are processed by Google Play through Google Play Billing. ReefSync does not receive or store payment card details or other financial credentials.

Google may process information related to purchases in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

## Advertising

ReefSync does not display third-party advertising.

## Data sharing

ReefSync does not sell users' personal information.

ReefSync does not intentionally share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by external service providers where this is technically necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, including Google Play services used for purchase processing.

## Data security

ReefSync is designed to minimize the collection and storage of personal information.

Where communication with external services is required, standard Android and network security mechanisms are used.

## Data retention and deletion

ReefSync does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database containing personal user profiles.

Locally stored app settings and data can be removed by clearing ReefSync's app data or uninstalling the application.

Purchase records handled by Google Play are subject to Google's applicable retention policies.

## Children's privacy

ReefSync is intended for adults and is not specifically designed for or directed at children.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ReefSync's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will remain available at this page.
