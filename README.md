# Privacy Policy – ReefSync

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the ReefSync Android application (“ReefSync”).

ReefSync is an aquarium lighting application designed to help reef aquarium hobbyists simulate natural reef lighting conditions and translate these into lighting schedules for supported aquarium lighting systems.

## Personal data

ReefSync does not require users to create an account or sign in.

ReefSync does not request access to the device's physical location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or messages.

ReefSync does not operate a developer-controlled user account database and does not use advertising or advertising identifiers.

## Reef location and simulation data

Users can enter or select a reef location whose natural lighting conditions they want to simulate.

This reef location is not the physical location of the user or their device.

To provide this functionality, ReefSync sends the entered reef-location search query to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API. The resulting reef coordinates are used with Open-Meteo services to retrieve information required for the lighting simulation, such as sunrise, sunset and solar radiation data.

These requests are made over HTTPS.

## Maps and external content

ReefSync uses OpenStreetMap-based map tiles to display the selected reef location.

The app may also retrieve certain images and other visual resources from external web servers, including NOAA and manufacturer websites.

When ReefSync connects to these external services, those services may necessarily receive technical network information such as the device's IP address and request information.

ReefSync does not use this information for advertising or to create advertising profiles.

## Local data

Aquarium settings, saved reef profiles and related ReefSync preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

ReefSync does not upload these locally saved profiles to a ReefSync-operated server.

Local app data can be removed by clearing ReefSync's app data or uninstalling the application.

## Google Play Billing

ReefSync offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock premium Export functionality.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. ReefSync does not receive or store payment card details or other financial credentials.

Google may process information related to purchases in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

## Advertising

ReefSync does not display third-party advertising.

## Data sharing

ReefSync does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to external services where technically necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, including Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap infrastructure, external content providers and Google Play services.

## Data security

Network requests made by ReefSync to the external services used by the app are made using encrypted HTTPS connections.

ReefSync is designed to minimize the collection and external transmission of user information.

## Data retention and deletion

ReefSync does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database containing personal user profiles.

Locally stored ReefSync data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling ReefSync.

Data processed independently by external service providers may be subject to their respective privacy and retention policies.

## Children's privacy

ReefSync is intended for adults and is not specifically designed for or directed at children.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ReefSync's functionality, data practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will remain available on this page.
